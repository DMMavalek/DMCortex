# Dungeon Master Codex / Player Codex v1.4.25

## Multiclass characters
- ADD EXISTING can record a different starting XP total for each class, including previously earned prime-requisite bonuses. Individual levels and historical CP awards use those totals.
- Base THAC0 now uses the best class progression at each class's own level. Ranger 8 provides base THAC0 13 even when another class is primary.
- Weapon attack rates use the warrior class's own level. A Ranger 8's specialized melee weapon correctly attacks twice per round; specialization is applied once per weapon.

## Proficiencies and Character Points
- Corrected Player's Option review and saved NWP summaries to grant +1 per paid improvement CP rather than dividing by acquisition cost.
- Added 37 source-attributed proficiency interactions, with free permanent bonuses separated from conditional targets and purchased improvements.
- Preserved Animal Lore/Animal Empathy Tracking conditions and added verified interactions for Set Snares, riding, navigation, crafts, history, and other proficiencies.
- Corrected free class/kit acquisition accounting and duplicate/stale improvement counting. Ranger class-granted Tracking appears automatically as a free NWP.
- Structured proficiency data preserves free-bonus provenance. Legacy targets without known improvement history remain intact, and repeated synchronization does not compound bonuses.

## Druid and Ranger abilities
- Corrected the fixed 60-CP Druid sphere package: major All, Animal, Elemental, Healing, Plant, and Weather; minor Divination.
- Added Druid Identify at 5 CP, with its benefit beginning at 3rd level.
- Ranger Special Enemy accepts a saved enemy choice; right-click the selected ability to edit it.
- Creature Languages (Druid) provides one free named creature language per Druid level, including level 1, using the Druid's own level for multiclass characters.
- Priest sphere summaries now show separate Major and Minor lists without garbled punctuation.

## Compatibility and limitations
- Release payloads exclude machine-local SMTP settings and ruleset/spell backup files.
- No automatic CP grants, refunds, or roster migration. Existing paid improvements and saved balances are retained.
- Automatic XP-award/prime-requisite bonus formulas and configured per-class CP advancement remain unchanged.
- The proficiency audit does not certify every supplemental handbook/custom definition. Restricted Intelligence allowance allocation, some ability-choice/custom-catalog differences, and broader cross-screen calculation parity remain under review.
